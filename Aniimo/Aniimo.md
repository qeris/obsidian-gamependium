# Aniimo

Free-to-play open-world creature collector from **Pawprint Studio** (a FunPlus label). Launched **September 16, 2026** on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S (day one on Game Pass); mobile followed September 23. Huge launch — 30M+ pre-registrations, ~168K peak Steam concurrents — but Steam reviews sit at Mixed, mostly over monetization.

You play a **Pathfinder** exploring the continent of **Idyll**, catching and bonding with 200+ magical creatures. See also: [[Aniimo - Beginner Guide]] and [[Aniimo - Tips and Tricks]].

## Core mechanics

> [!info] Twining
> The signature mechanic: merge with a captured creature and **control it directly**, using its elemental powers, attacks, and traversal abilities (swim, climb, glide, fly). A creature's value isn't just combat — its Twine abilities can unlock new areas and solve puzzles.

- **Catching** — Enter Catch Mode and throw **Aniipods**. Spawns depend on biome, weather, phenomena, and time of day. Back-strike (throw from behind) boosts catch rate by 150%.
- **Evolution** — Creatures evolve (taught early in the story, e.g. Lunara → Fennelun).
- **Prismana** — Rainbow-sheened rare variants of 22 species. Triggered via **Wild Surges** and **Prismana Flow**; Nurturing Blooms raises regional Prismana Energy.
- **Homeland** — Personal basebuilding (unlocks at character level 20): farming, mining, and **Hatchinators** that incubate eggs over real-time hours.
- **Combat roles** — Team of DPS / Break / Regen / Heal (+ Support). Elemental **and** Spatial counters matter (e.g. Tunnel to dodge ranged attacks).
- **Character Talents** — Your Pathfinder's own skills (heal, buff, attack), used in Command Mode alongside Twining.

## Currencies & resources

| Currency | Notes |
|---|---|
| Credits | Main free currency |
| Aniipods | Catching ammo (standard, Pro, **Tumbler** — controllable, 2x catch rate, 6x with nurture) |
| Glimmer | Upgrade material |
| Primegy | Energy/stamina for activities — **don't let it cap** |
| Sparkling Aniipods | Progression currency |
| Lumin Crystals | Paid currency — beginners should hold off spending |

## Social & live-service

- Personal **RV + campsite** as your social hub; a **sky-floating metropolis** as the central battle/event hub.
- Always-online; daily/weekly tasks, rotating events (some creatures are event-exclusive), Twitch Drops at launch.
- PS5 players: server is tied to your PSN account region.
