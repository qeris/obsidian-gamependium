# Aniimo — Beginner Guide

Companion to [[Aniimo]]. Focus: your first hours and first weekend. Mechanics deep-dives live in [[Aniimo - Tips and Tricks]].

## 1. Pick your starter carefully

> [!warning] Permanent choice
> You choose between **Lunara** and **Helion** at the start, your rival keeps the other, and there is **no confirmed way to catch the one you didn't pick later**. Same base stats (90 HP / 116 ATK / 85 Break / 65 M.DEF / 65 P.DEF / 83 Regen) and same Hustle mobility — the difference is playstyle, not power.
> - **Lunara** — ranged, sustain-leaning. Fox Mark: Lumin Bolts shreds Light resistance and heals on marked hits. The forgiving pick; evolves into **Fennelun**.
> - **Helion** — the close-range alternative.
>
> Guide consensus leans Lunara for newcomers. Either way, your starter stays viable into endgame — invest in it with confidence.

Source: [Lunara vs Helion — GamesHorizon](https://gameshorizon.com/guides/lunara-vs-helion-which-aniimo-starter-to-pick/)

## 2. Learn Twining immediately

Twining isn't a gimmick — it's half the game. Test **every** new catch while Twined: its best ability might be swimming, climbing, gliding, or a puzzle interaction rather than combat power. New areas frequently require a specific Twined ability to reach.

Source: [Beginner guide — G2A](https://www.g2a.com/news/features/guide/aniimo-beginner-guide/)

## 3. Catch smart, not hard

- Enter **Catch Mode**, sneak **behind** the creature, throw. Back strike = +150% catch rate and usually skips combat.
- **Missed Aniipods return to you automatically** — throw freely.
- Carry spares and watch conditions: spawns shift with **weather, time of day, and phenomena**. If something won't appear, come back under different conditions instead of grinding the same route.
- Early utility pickups: the **Tumbler** (controllable pod, 2x catch rate), the **Tweetin' Target** skill (rubber-duck distraction), and **Bubble Gun** (keeps a target above 1 HP for more capture attempts).

Sources: [Capturing Guide — Game8](https://game8.co/games/Aniimo/archives/620560), [8 catching/exploration tips — Game Pelt](https://gamepelt.com/aniimo/aniimo-8-catching-exploration-tips)

## 4. Level via the Elite Program

Main-story quests get **level-gated**. The fastest character EXP comes from **Dr. Lewis's Elite Program** objectives — do capture tasks first, since a first-time catch grants big bonus EXP on its own.

Source: [15 Essential Beginner Tips — Game8](https://game8.co/games/aniimo/archives/620172)

## 5. Unlock Homeland at level 20, start it early

Homeland (your base) unlocks at character level 20. Farming, mining, and egg incubation run on **real-time hours** — the earlier you start upgrades and Hatchinators, the more they compound. Build several Hatchinators and keep them running.

## 6. Build a balanced team early

Aim for **DPS + Break + Regen + Heal** coverage rather than stacking favorites. Save team presets per element — swap on the fly with **H** (PC) or hold **Triangle/Y + L1** (consoles).

## 7. Daily rhythm (don't cap your resources)

- **Spend Primegy** on activities daily — never let it sit capped.
- **Nurture Blooms** (3 free attempts/day at the Breezy Plains Branch) to trigger Wild Surges / Prismana Flow.
- Check the **events tab** daily — some creatures are event-exclusive.
- Weeklies: **Omega Aniimo** fights, buy out limited vendor stock (e.g. Joff).
- Grab **Lumin Ambers** while exploring and turn them in at the region Branch — perks include unlimited exploration stamina and Prismana Crystals.

Source: [15 Essential Beginner Tips — Game8](https://game8.co/games/aniimo/archives/620172)
